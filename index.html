Privacy Policy for Simple Scanner

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sidharth Gupta ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you use the Simple Scanner mobile application (the "App").

1. Data Collection and Usage

Simple Scanner is designed around our core philosophy: Fast, Secure, and Simple. Your privacy is our highest priority.

  • Offline Processing: All document scanning, image enhancement, and PDF generation processes occur completely locally on your device. We do not transmit, upload, or store your scanned documents on any external servers.
  • No Personal Information Collected: We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personal identifiers, email addresses, phone numbers, or analytics.

2. Permissions Required

To function properly, the App requires the following device permissions:

  • Camera Permission: Required to capture images of your physical documents.
  • Storage Access (Scoped Storage): Required to save the generated PDF and JPG files to the specific directory you select on your device.

We do not access any files, photos, or directories outside of the specific folder you explicitly grant the App permission to use via the Android Storage Access Framework.

3. Third-Party Services

The App utilizes Google ML Kit Document Scanner API to provide fast auto-cropping, edge detection, and image enhancement features. While ML Kit processes your images locally on your device, it is a Google service and may collect basic, anonymous usage metrics (such as the number of times a scan is initiated) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. It does not upload your images or documents to Google servers.

4. Data Retention

Since we do not collect or transmit your data, we have no data to retain. Your scanned documents remain securely on your device until you manually delete them.

5. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
