Privacy Policy for Simple Scanner

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sidharth Gupta ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Simple Scanner mobile application (the "App"). Our philosophy is built on absolute privacy: Fast, Secure, and Simple.

1. Zero Data Collection & Local Processing

Simple Scanner operates entirely offline. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, documents, or usage analytics.

  • On-Device Processing: All document cropping, image enhancement, compression, and PDF generation occur locally on your device hardware.
  • No Account Required: We do not ask for personal identifiers, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No Telemetry or Trackers: The App contains no third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or crash-reporting telemetry that transmits your data to us.

2. App Permissions

To operate securely and maintain your privacy, the App strictly limits the permissions it requests:

  • No Camera Permission Required: The App itself does not request or require Camera permissions. Document capture is securely delegated to the native Google Play Services scanner on your device (see Section 3).
  • Scoped Storage Access: The App only requests permission to save generated PDF and JPG files to specific, user-designated directories using the Android Storage Access Framework. We cannot and do not access files, photos, or directories outside of the specific output folder you explicitly grant us access to.

3. Third-Party Services (Google ML Kit)

To provide advanced edge detection and image enhancement without requiring direct camera access, the App utilizes the Google ML Kit Document Scanner API. When you initiate a scan, the App delegates the camera capture process to Google Play Services.

  • The document processing is performed locally on your device by Google Play Services.
  • While ML Kit processes images locally, it is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, which may collect basic, anonymized metrics (such as API call frequency) for system health.
  • Your scanned documents and images are never uploaded to Google servers or accessible by us.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Simple Scanner does not collect or transmit any user data, we have no data to retain or delete on our end. Your documents remain strictly under your control on your device's local storage until you manually delete them.

5. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Because we do not collect contact information, you are encouraged to review this page periodically.
