1. Zero Data Collection & Offline-Only Architecture

Simple Scanner operates locally on your device for document scanning, editing, compression, viewing, and file management. We do not operate user-data servers, and the App does not declare the Android INTERNET permission or collect, store, transmit, sell, or share your personal information, scanned documents, or usage data.

  • On-Device Processing: Camera capture, edge detection, cropping, auto-deskew, enhancement filters, JPEG/PDF compression, watermarking, PDF encryption, and built-in document viewing execute locally on your device.
  • No Accounts Required: The App does not require sign-in, user registration, email addresses, phone numbers, or personal identifiers.
  • No Third-Party Ads, Analytics, or Crash SDKs: We do not bundle any third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral trackers, remote configuration services, or third-party analytics/crash-reporting SDKs in the App. Platform-level Google Play services (such as Play Store licensing, in-app updates, and optional device-level Play/Android diagnostics) are operated solely by Google as described in Section 4.
  • Local Privacy Tools: By default, the App is configured to strip EXIF, XMP, IPTC, and GPS metadata from exported JPEGs and clear document metadata from generated PDFs (configurable on the Save screen). Passwords you enter to encrypt or unlock PDFs are processed locally in memory only and are never saved by the App or transmitted.

2. App Permissions & Scoped Local File Access

All file access performed by the App is strictly local functionality on your device; no files or metadata are collected or shared with us. The App requests or includes only the permissions required for user-initiated functionality, bundled Android libraries, or Google Play distribution:

  • Camera Permission (CAMERA): Requested only when you use the built-in camera scanner to capture pages. Captured frames are processed locally on your device. If you decline camera access, you can still import and edit existing images and PDFs from your device.
  • Scoped Storage Access (MediaStore & Storage Access Framework): The App does not request broad all-files access. By default, exported JPGs and PDFs are written locally via Android MediaStore to Documents/Simple Scanner (Images/ and PDFs/). If you choose a custom save folder, the App uses the Android Storage Access Framework to access only the directory you explicitly select.
  • User-Selected Imports: When you select images or PDFs through the Android system photo/file picker, or share/open files into the App from another application, the App accesses only the specific files you select.
  • View Network Connections (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Automatically merged by bundled Android Jetpack/CameraX library components to allow the operating system to inspect local connectivity state. Because the App does not declare the INTERNET permission, Simple Scanner cannot open network connections to upload your documents or personal data.
  • Google Play License Check (CHECK_LICENSE): Automatically added by Google Play during app store distribution to allow Google Play on your device to verify app installation and update integrity. We do not collect or receive any data from this check, and it has no access to your documents, filenames, or personal information.

3. Local Caches, Preferences & Backups

  • Temporary Processing Cache: During active capture, cropping, enhancement, rendering, and compression, temporary working files and thumbnails are stored inside the App's private cache directory and are normally deleted automatically when you save, discard a session, or close a document viewer session. However, if the App crashes, is force-closed, or loses power unexpectedly, residual temporary files may remain in your device's private cache until cleared or until the App is uninstalled.
  • Local Preferences: App settings (such as default JPEG quality, startup behavior, view/sort mode, and export preferences) are saved locally on your device via Android Jetpack DataStore.
  • Backup Exclusion Configuration: Local DataStore preferences (including custom folder URI references) are configured in our manifest backup rules to be excluded from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfers, and private app caches are not backed up by Android Auto Backup. Persisted Storage Access Framework URI grants are managed locally by the Android system per device. Your operating system, device manufacturer, or third-party backup tools may still back up other shared device directories independently according to your device settings.

4. Third-Party Libraries, Google Play Services & External Apps

  • Embedded Local Libraries: Document detection, image processing, compression, and PDF generation/rendering use local libraries (including AndroidX CameraX, OpenCV, jpegli, Android PdfRenderer, and Apache PDFBox-Android) without sending files to our servers or cloud scanning services.
  • Google Play Platform Services: When installed via Google Play, Google Play may perform automated license verification (CHECK_LICENSE), the App integrates the Google Play In-App Update API to check through the Google Play Store app on your device whether a newer version is available, and Google Play or the Android OS may collect system-level diagnostics (such as Android vitals) depending on your device and Google account settings. These platform-level services are operated and governed solely by Google under Google's Privacy Policy, not by the developers of Simple Scanner, and the App itself never passes your documents, filenames, or personal data to them.
  • Sharing & External Apps: When you choose to share a file or open it externally, Android transfers access to the selected file to the external app or service you pick. Once shared or opened outside the App, that file is governed exclusively by the privacy policy and security practices of the receiving app or service.

5. Shared Storage Realities & User Responsibilities

While we take utmost care in designing Simple Scanner for local-only operation, privacy and security also depend on your device environment and how you handle exported files. You acknowledge and agree that you are solely responsible for:

  • Shared Device Storage: Files saved to Documents/Simple Scanner or a user-selected folder reside in shared device storage. Other apps on your device that hold storage permissions, system media scanners, gallery apps, or cloud-sync services enabled on your device (such as Google Drive, Photos, or manufacturer cloud backups) may be able to read, index, or sync those files outside of our control.
  • Verifying Sensitive Exports: Always inspect exported files before submitting or sharing sensitive documents to verify that cropping, redactions, watermarks, EXIF metadata removal, and PDF password encryption meet your requirements.
  • Password Management: Because the App does not store or transmit your PDF passwords, lost or forgotten passwords cannot be recovered by us.
  • Device Security: Maintaining device passcodes, OS security updates, and physical device security, and recognizing that compromised, malware-infected, rooted, or modified devices may expose local app memory, caches, or storage to unauthorized third parties.
